# Source Boundary Notes

This file summarizes source-use boundaries for the public repository bundle.

- PV field case: public NREL/OEDI PVDAQ system 2107, cited in the manuscript.
- PV monitoring framing: IEC 61724-1:2021, cited in the manuscript.
- SWaT structural pilot: raw SWaT A4/A5 July 2019 historian data are controlled by iTrust and are not redistributed here.
- SWaT publication references: Goh et al. dataset paper and Adepu & Mathur invariant paper, cited in the manuscript and supplementary material.
- Transfer examples: AMPds2, Kitakyushu campus energy data, UK-DALE, REFIT, Sønderborg district heating, and OpenDHN are cited in the manuscript as source-backed settings, not as additional numerical validation.
